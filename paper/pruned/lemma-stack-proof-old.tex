The cells of stack $s$ that lie in band $b$ are exactly the box where band $b$ and stack $s$ cross. By the box
constraint $\sigma$ puts $d$ in exactly one cell $y_b$ of that box, so band $b$ has exactly one $d$ in the stack's
columns, and $\tau$ is well defined. The stack's cells are the union of its three boxes, so $y_0,y_1,y_2$ are all the
cells of the stack holding $d$; by the column constraint each of its three columns holds exactly one of them, so $\tau$
is a bijection. Consistency gives $(y_b,d)\in C$, so $B^{d,s}_{b\tau(b)}=1$. The rest is as in \cref{lem:band}.
